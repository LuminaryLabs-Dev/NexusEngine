# 0.0.5 Physics Body

`0.0.5` proves `n:physics:body` as the canonical owner of portable provider-neutral body identity, pose, velocity, mass properties, force state, damping, sleep/wake state, lifecycle, and exact-once registry records.

## Ownership

Own portable provider-neutral body identity, state, mass properties, sleep state, lifecycle, and exact-once registry mutations.

It owns:

- body force and mass-property descriptors
- body identity and type
- body pose and velocity descriptors
- body records and revisions
- body sleep and lifecycle state

It does not own:

- actor or gameplay state
- collision detection or contact solving
- motion integration
- provider-native body handles
- shape or collider geometry

## Atomic Kits

- `body-damping-kit`: Normalize finite nonnegative linear and angular damping descriptors.
- `body-force-kit`: Normalize portable force, torque, and impulse accumulator descriptors.
- `body-identity-kit`: Normalize stable portable Physics body identity, tags, and metadata.
- `body-inertia-kit`: Normalize principal inertia, inverse inertia, and local inertia orientation.
- `body-lifecycle-kit`: Normalize active and disabled body lifecycle state and exact transition commands.
- `body-mass-kit`: Normalize body mass, inverse mass, and center-of-mass descriptors.
- `body-pose-kit`: Normalize body position and canonical quaternion orientation.
- `body-registry-kit`: Own portable Physics body records and exact-once lifecycle transitions without solver execution.
- `body-sleep-kit`: Normalize body sleep state and explicit exact-once sleep commands.
- `body-state-kit`: Compose atomic portable body descriptors into one coherent provider-neutral body state.
- `body-type-kit`: Normalize static, dynamic, and kinematic Physics body modes.
- `body-velocity-kit`: Normalize finite linear and angular Physics body velocity descriptors.
- `body-wake-kit`: Normalize explicit exact-once Physics body wake commands.

## Proof

Direct behavior, lifecycle/composition where applicable, failure handling, portability, and backend-boundary behavior are exercised by `tests/core-domains/core-physics-body-smoke.mjs`.

The Core records remain provider-neutral: concrete Rapier, PhysX, Three.js, WebGL, WebGPU, native-window, and GPU-resource handles stay outside this semantic boundary.
