# 0.0.5 Physics Shape

`0.0.5` proves `n:physics:shape` as the canonical owner of portable collision-shape identity, validation, primitive geometry, convex/mesh/heightfield data, compound composition, and scaling semantics.

## Ownership

Own portable provider-neutral collision-shape identity, geometry descriptors, validation, and exact-once registration.

It owns:

- compound and scaled shape composition
- mesh and heightfield collision descriptors
- primitive geometry descriptors
- shape identity
- shape validation and registry state

It does not own:

- authored level geometry
- collision detection
- contact generation or solving
- provider shape objects
- render geometry or GPU resources

## Atomic Kits

- `box-shape-kit`: Normalize portable box collision geometry.
- `capsule-shape-kit`: Normalize portable capsule collision geometry.
- `compound-shape-kit`: Normalize portable compound collision-shape references and local poses.
- `cone-shape-kit`: Normalize portable cone collision geometry.
- `convex-shape-kit`: Normalize portable convex-hull collision geometry.
- `cylinder-shape-kit`: Normalize portable cylinder collision geometry.
- `heightfield-shape-kit`: Normalize sampled portable heightfield collision geometry.
- `shape-identity-kit`: Normalize stable portable Physics shape identity, type, and metadata.
- `plane-shape-kit`: Normalize portable infinite-plane collision geometry.
- `shape-registry-kit`: Own exact-once deterministic registration and lookup of portable Physics shapes.
- `scaled-shape-kit`: Normalize portable positive nonuniform scaling of a referenced collision shape.
- `sphere-shape-kit`: Normalize portable sphere collision geometry.
- `triangle-mesh-shape-kit`: Normalize indexed portable triangle-mesh collision geometry.
- `shape-validation-kit`: Validate any canonical portable Physics shape descriptor without mutation.

## Proof

Direct behavior, lifecycle/composition where applicable, failure handling, portability, and backend-boundary behavior are exercised by `tests/core-domains/core-physics-shape-smoke.mjs`.

The Core records remain provider-neutral: concrete Rapier, PhysX, Three.js, WebGL, WebGPU, native-window, and GPU-resource handles stay outside this semantic boundary.
