# Cycle 009 Goal

Complete the `n:physics/material` master package as six canonical, atomic,
provider-neutral Kits with exact committed evidence for all 42 detailed actions.

## Included

- Physical material identity and immutable registry records.
- Friction, restitution, density, and physical surface descriptors.
- Deterministic symmetric material-combine policy.
- Strict portable snapshots, exact-once mutation, and read-only queries.
- Nested public exports, generated catalogs, documentation, migration, package,
  and clean-consumer proof.

## Excluded

- Visual materials, shaders, textures, decals, and authored effects.
- Audio surface effects and gameplay surface rules.
- Bodies, colliders, contact generation, solver impulses, or provider execution.
- Consumer changes, pushes, releases, and numeric branch mutation.

The broader mission remains unchanged: prove Physics and Render, publish the
separately approved immutable `0.0.5` snapshot, leave `main` ready for `0.0.6`,
and repeatedly validate The Open Above against lock-resolved `main`.
