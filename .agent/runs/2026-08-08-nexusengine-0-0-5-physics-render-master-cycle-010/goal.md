provider-neutral Kits with exact committed evidence for all 49 detailed actions.

- Portable Physics world identity and immutable world records.
- Coordinate, units, bounds, and out-of-bounds settings.
- Deterministic gravity, generic force, and physical wind field sampling.
- Physics-only time scales and physical simulation activation regions.

- Runtime scheduling, clocks, lifecycle stepping, bodies, colliders, contacts,
  solving, or concrete provider execution.
- Visual wind effects, particles, shaders, audio, and gameplay rules.
- Consumer changes, pushes, releases, and numeric branch mutation.

The broader mission remains unchanged: prove Physics and Render, publish the
separately approved immutable `0.0.5` snapshot, leave `main` ready for `0.0.6`,
and repeatedly validate The Open Above against lock-resolved `main`.
